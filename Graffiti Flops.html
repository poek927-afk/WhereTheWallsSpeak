Flop

Thought of as a more sophisticated version of the tag and much more visible, the “throw up” or “flop” is a form of lettering executed just as quickly, but which takes the name of the graffiti artist (or “writer”) and enlarges it. The goal is to become known with a more impressive style because the letters are larger. The tagger usually uses two colors, one for filling the letters and the other one for the outline. The letter is transformed by adding volume, often achieved through a style known as “bubble”. New York graffiti artists in the 1970s competed using this technique to obtain the honorary title of “All City King.”
