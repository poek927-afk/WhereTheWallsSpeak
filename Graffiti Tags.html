Tags

A must for all graffiti artists, the “tag” is the starting point of the Graffiti movement. A real signature made up of stylized letters that form a name (the pseudonym of the artist or his crew), the tag is made at full speed, usually using a single color, and can be small or large depending on the material used. Some tags are sometimes barely readable and need to be decoded, read and deciphered by the more initiated. Taggers, as they are usually called, never stop competing, inventing new forms, playing with letters, distorting them, stretching them to offer a language that has evolved over the years.
